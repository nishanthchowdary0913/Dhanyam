01 / The idea

In organic farming,
the soil comes first.

Organic paddy farming is more than avoiding chemicals. It protects biodiversity, strengthens the soil, and keeps farming safe for every living thing. Our CSP project connects field observation with practical learning for the farming community.

02 / Paddy Journey

From a Tiny Seed
to a Golden Harvest

Every grain of rice tells a story of patience, care, and dedication. The journey of paddy cultivation begins with healthy soil and quality seeds, continues through careful planting and natural crop protection, and ends with a golden harvest.

By following sustainable farming practices, farmers can improve soil fertility, protect the environment, and produce healthy food for future generations.

01 / Prepare

Build Healthy Soil

Farmers plough and level the field while maintaining suitable moisture. Organic compost, decomposed farmyard manure, and other natural materials improve fertility and support beneficial soil microorganisms.

02 / Plant

Give Every Seed a Beginning

Healthy native or traditional seeds are selected according to local soil, climate, water availability, and season. Careful sowing, proper spacing, and suitable water management help seedlings establish strong roots.

03 / Protect

Grow with Nature

Regular crop monitoring, field cleanliness, beneficial insects, botanical methods, balanced soil fertility, and proper water management help protect paddy while reducing unnecessary chemical use.

04 / Harvest

Celebrate the Golden Reward

When grains reach maturity and develop their golden colour, farmers harvest, dry, clean, and store them properly. Suitable seeds can be saved for the next season, continuing the farming cycle.

A cycle that sustains the future

Paddy cultivation is a continuous relationship between farmers, soil, water, seeds, and nature. Traditional knowledge and sustainable methods support soil health, conserve resources, and carry each season’s lessons into the next harvest.
