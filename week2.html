Community Service Project

Week 2 Report

Learning about natural manure, organic inputs and practical paddy-field work.

DayActivityObservations / Notes
Day 1Visited paddy fields and met farmers.Observed the field conditions and discussed the importance of natural inputs in organic paddy cultivation.
Day 2Observed the collection of natural manure.Studied how buffalo dung and other organic material are collected and prepared for field use.
Day 3Learned about handling and carrying manure.Observed the practical work involved in moving prepared manure from the collection area to the paddy field.
Day 4Observed preparation of a natural liquid input.Worked with farmers near the field water source and learned how organic inputs are mixed and made ready for application.
Day 5Recorded field learning and evidence.Discussed how manure and natural inputs improve soil fertility, support crop growth and reduce dependence on chemical inputs.
