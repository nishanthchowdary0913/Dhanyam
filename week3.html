Community Service Project

Week 3 Report

Observing paddy growth, panicle development and crop health in the field.

DayActivityObservations / Notes
Day 1Observed paddy panicles and leaves.Examined the crop at close range and noted the stage of panicle development.
Day 2Compared crop growth in different field areas.Observed variation in plant colour, leaf condition and grain formation across the paddy field.
Day 3Inspected mature paddy plants with a farmer.Learned how farmers check grain filling, plant strength and overall crop health.
Day 4Observed signs of crop stress.Studied damaged leaves and drying portions so that field problems can be identified early.
Day 5Recorded crop observations.Understood that regular monitoring of panicles, leaves and water conditions supports timely organic crop care.
