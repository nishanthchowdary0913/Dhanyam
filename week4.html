Community Service Project

Week 4 Report

Learning irrigation, drainage and careful water management in paddy cultivation.

DayActivityObservations / Notes
Day 1Observed field water channels.Studied how water moves through the channel beside the paddy field and reaches the crop area.
Day 2Participated in cleaning the water channel.Worked with the team to remove blockages and understand the importance of keeping irrigation paths clear.
Day 3Observed water entering the paddy field.Noted the flow of water between crop rows and how farmers maintain suitable field moisture.
Day 4Checked drainage and outlet points.Learned how controlled drainage prevents excess water from damaging the paddy crop.
Day 5Recorded irrigation observations.Understood that timely water supply, proper channels and careful drainage are essential for healthy organic paddy production.
