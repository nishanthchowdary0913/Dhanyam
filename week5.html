Community Service Project

Week 5 Report

Studying compost, natural inputs and farmer-led crop protection practices.

DayActivityObservations / Notes
Day 1Visited the compost and manure preparation area.Observed the storage of straw, dung and decomposed organic material used for soil improvement.
Day 2Studied compost preparation.Learned how organic material is handled and prepared before it is taken to the paddy field.
Day 3Observed preparation of a natural crop input.Watched farmers measure and mix the input with water near the field channel.
Day 4Observed field application.Learned how a prepared input is applied across the paddy crop using a sprayer.
Day 5Discussed organic crop protection with farmers.Recorded how manure, careful input preparation and timely field application support crop health.
