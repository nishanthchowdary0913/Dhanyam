Week 6 Report

Learning through crop inspection, farmer guidance and field-level observation.

DayActivityObservations / Notes
Day 1Examined paddy plants with a farmer.Observed the crop closely and discussed plant growth, panicle formation and field condition.

Day 3Discussed crop problems and field care.Learned from the farmer’s practical experience while checking the paddy plants.
Day 4Observed group field assessment.Worked with farmers and team members to examine different portions of the crop and share observations.
Day 5Recorded learning for the project.Organised photographs and notes showing how regular crop inspection supports organic paddy farming.
